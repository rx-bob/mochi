# Third-party components

This archive includes the license texts for the compiler's
[Tree-sitter](https://github.com/yampug/tree-sitter),
[Tree-sitter Ruby](https://github.com/yampug/tree-sitter-ruby), and
[Sorbet](https://github.com/yampug/sorbet) dependencies. It also includes
the mruby license and notices supplied by the Crystal and Homebrew
installations used to build it.

The GVM binary is from the accompanying Mochi GVM build. Its license and notices
are included when supplied by that checkout.
